Global Temperatures Hit Record Highs in May 2026

Scientists warn of accelerating climate impacts as new data emerges from the World Meteorological Organization.

The latest report shows average global temperatures soared past previous records by a significant margin.

Extreme weather events, including heatwaves and flooding, have increased dramatically in vulnerable regions around the world.

Experts are calling for urgent international policy action ahead of the major climate summit next month.

Data visualizations reveal stark regional differences, with polar areas warming at twice the global average rate.
